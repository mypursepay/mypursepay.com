JOB DESCRIPTION — SALES & MERCHANT ACQUISITION ASSOCIATE

Role: Sales & Merchant Acquisition Associate
Type: Remote
Location: Nationwide (online outreach)


READ THIS CAREFULLY — NO CONFUSION, NO EXCUSES

Welcome on board. Before you start, understand exactly what this job is and what it is not.

You’re being hired as a Sales & Merchant Acquisition Associate for our e-commerce platform (a Nigerian alternative to Shopify).

Your job is simple: bring in merchants who currently sell on Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, etc. We target fashion vendors, hair/wig sellers, gadget sellers, mini-importers, small business owners, and similar online vendors.

What merchants get

  • Their own e-commerce website
  • NGN payment integration (we do not take a cut — merchants keep 100% of sales)
  • Cart + order management
  • Wallet (for high volume sellers)
  • Marketplace listing (like Amazon)
  • Store setup in 10 minutes
  • Free plan + affordable upgrades

Your responsibilities

  • Find and reach out to vendors using your own social accounts (IG, TikTok, WhatsApp, Facebook).
  • Get them to create a store on the platform.
  • Help with setup if they need guidance.
  • Convert them to paid account upgrades (monthly Pro or yearly Plus).
  • Follow up consistently until they convert.
  • Hit daily and monthly targets.

This is a results job. No results = no reason to stay.

Compensation (final & accurate)

Base salary: NGN 100,000 per month.

Commissions (YEARLY ONLY):

  • Plus (NGN 60,000 / year) → NGN 5,000 commission per Plus yearly upgrade.
  • No commission on monthly Pro subscriptions.

Internet/data bonus: If you exceed your monthly target, you get NGN10,000 extra for data.

Monthly performance bonuses

Bonus tiers based on the number of paid upgrades you close in a month:

  • Level 0: 0–20 → No bonus
  • Level 1: 21–40 → Commission only
  • Level 2: 41–60 → +NGN 15,000 bonus
  • Level 3: 61–90 → +NGN 40,000 bonus
  • Level 4: 91–120 → +NGN 75,000 bonus
  • Level 5: 120+ → +NGN 150,000 bonus

(Internet allowance applies from Level 2 upward.)

Targets (UPDATED — exact)

  • Monthly minimum: 15 account upgrades (this includes monthly Pro upgrades and yearly Plus upgrades).
  • Out of those 15, at least 5 must be yearly pro upgrades (NGN 30k/year).

If you consistently miss your monthly target, your contract will be discontinued. No excuses.

Work style

  • Fully remote.
  • You choose your hours.
  • Use your own accounts for outreach.
  • We provide flyers, design assets, onboarding videos, and scripts.
  • We do NOT provide spreadsheets, accounts, or messaging templates — you’re expected to own your process.
  • You are judged 100% on results.

Promotion

Hit Level 3 or higher for 3 consecutive months and you become Senior Sales Associate with:

  • Salary: NGN 150,000
  • Renewal commission: NGN 500 per returning merchant (on renewals)
  • Override commission: NGN 300 per upgrade from trainees (first 2 months of each trainee)

Support provided

  • Onboarding videos (including “How to set up a store in 10 minutes”)
  • Product FAQs and knowledge base
  • Example messages/scripts and promo materials

You provide the effort.

Daily expectations

  • 50–80 outreach messages/day
  • Track everyone you speak with and maintain follow-ups until conversion
  • Push yearly Plus plans (higher value for company & bigger commission for you)
  • Stay consistent and disciplined

Lazy people don’t make money here. Consistent people do.

Final note

The platform can handle 1,000+ merchants/day, so volume is NOT an issue. Your job is to bring the merchants in, guide them, and close upgrades.
